Does it hear me?

Ten hand-labelled clips of me addressing Jarvis, plus negatives, so a later test can ask two things: can a wake check tell addressing Jarvis from mentioning it, and can it tell me live from me replayed. Each clip is five seconds; the context capture keeps the five seconds before the button as well.

This page only collects. Saved clips sync to your Mac for local speaker-reference diagnostics on the existing 30-minute pull schedule. There is no liveness or intent classifier and no deploy switch. The microphone starts only when you press the button and stops when this page is hidden.

This browser does not expose getUserMedia or Web Audio, so nothing here can run. Open the page in Safari or Chrome over HTTPS.

Recommended sequence for carl_live

Ten clips in this session: seven fit, then three test. The guided sequence switches the split for you before the next capture. Same-session tests are exploratory, not independent validation; collect later-session tests and other-speaker examples too.

Draft prompts

Written by the agent for this study, not Carl's own statements. Say them in your own words if you prefer, then write what you actually said in the note. Never say card numbers, account numbers, passwords or anything you would not want kept: the clip is stored.

One session per sitting and per speaker. Use a fresh session when someone else records other_live, or when you switch to carl_replay.

3 · capture

5.0seconds

Capture context keeps the five seconds already in memory and records five more, with a marker at 5.0 s. Press it right as you say the wake word. The marker is when you pressed, not where the word is; exact wake alignment is not claimed. It unlocks once five seconds of live audio are buffered.

Audio stays in this page's memory until you press Save. Nothing is written anywhere by recording alone.

Held-out vs fit

fittest (held out)
Identity is not liveness. A clip that sounds like Carl is not proof that Carl is in the room; carl_replay exists to measure exactly that gap. Nothing is claimed about either until the held-out set is scored.
Fit and test stay separate. The Mac can build a provisional reference from at least three fit-only carl_live clips. Its fixed 0.60 threshold is uncalibrated; it is not an authorization rule. Replays and mentions are reported separately, never mistaken for proof of live intent. Nothing enables Jarvis automatically.
